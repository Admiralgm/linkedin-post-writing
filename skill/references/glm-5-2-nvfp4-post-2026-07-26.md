## Key Lessons

1. **wc -m counts characters including newlines** — a 3000-char limit includes
   all whitespace. Plan for ~2800 chars of visible text + 200 chars of newlines.

2. **Iterative trimming with patch is cleaner than full rewrite** — when over
   the character limit, make 2-3 targeted `patch` edits (remove a word here,
   shorten a phrase there) rather than rewriting the whole post.

3. **User "expand to near 3000" means add substance, not padding** — the user
   wants opinion paragraphs, technical details, and personal closers. NOT
   filler words or redundant restatements.

4. **Multi-draft merge = synthesis, not concatenation** — read all drafts,
   identify the best element from each, then write a NEW post that
   incorporates them as a single coherent narrative.

5. **FINISH FINISH FINISH causes system truncation** — keep the summary after
   FINISH very short (1-2 lines) to avoid the system cutting it off and
   generating repeated empty continuation messages.
